01

Look

Take their discipline, not their photorealism. Toon bands and ink outlines are why you can tell nkgta from one frame. What New York has that we lack is time, weather, and a rule that nothing ever shimmers.

Nothing shrinks below a pixel

Their rule: every procedural detail fades to its average before it gets smaller than a pixel. Windows, brick bands, road cracks, dashes. Left is a plain window grid, right is theirs, both drawn by the same shader. Pull the zoom out and watch the left side boil.

drag the canvas to move the split

The trick is fwidth: how many window cells one pixel covers. A box-filtered pulse turns that into exact coverage, and the per-window lamp fades to the share of lamps that are on. Our overhead camera sweeps the whole Kiez at every zoom, so this is the exact problem, everywhere. tsl-materials.js:148 filteredPulse · :166 distantWindowLight

Time of day is twenty numbers

Their four presets are one bundle: sun, sky, fog, plus a few numbers every material reads. interiorGlow means "how night is it", so windows, lamps and the river's reflected skyline all follow one dial. Ours would be a toon version: bands stay, the colours move. Hover the street to be the player; only the lamps nearest you get a pool of light.

02

Life

Our traffic is already good: rechts vor links, greens rolling down Karl-Marx-Straße, convoys. What's missing is a street that notices you. New York's people dive out of the way and its drivers swerve, late and a bit badly. That's GTA in eighty lines.

Dive, swerve, shout

Drive the yellow car: arrow keys or WASD after clicking the street, or hold the mouse where you want to go. People look a second and a half ahead, try stopping, sprinting, turning back or diving, and keep whichever leaves the most room. Drivers do the same with brake, gas and a swerve, and commit to it.

Ours already throws drivers out and has them shout "Ey! Mein Auto!". Add the dive and the swerve and the street stops being a backdrop. pedestrian-system.js:296–355 · pedestrians.js:968 the dive · traffic.js:1124 react()

Hear the Hinterhof

New York casts eight probes at 2 Hz against its building bounds to guess how boxed in you are, and sends every sound to one shared reverb by that much. Sources behind walls get three rays each and a lowpass. We have it cheaper: the walk grid is already a map of walls. Move the mouse through the Durchfahrt into the courtyards, and turn the Späti radio on.

Their version: audio-acoustics.js:44–97 · audio-system.js:13 one convolver for all. Ours would read SOLID tiles along the ray, so a Durchfahrt or a Hof sounds like one with no extra data.

03

Play

Here New York has nothing to teach. It's a superb place with no game in it, which leaves our lead. Pfand, shops you walk into, cars you steal, convoys you hear coming, a Kiez that sings. The long game is to make that loop deep.

What we leave in New York

Leave

  • Mode buttons 1–4 and teleports. Getting a car by pressing 2 kills the best verb we have: taking one.
  • Panel HUD. Speedo, camera picker, toolbar. Our screen is pictures, only when needed. Keep it that way.
  • Photoreal. It's handsome stock; it also shows how rough OSM is up close. Toon and ink forgive it.
  • Eleven cameras. One overhead, one chase, and the drone for menus and replays.
  • The 1,700-line gait rig, for now. From above, people are eight pixels tall.

Take

  • The rules: fade to the mean, one night uniform, budgets per device, zero work on idle frames.
  • The reactions: dive, swerve, honk, chatter.
  • The ears: enclosure reverb and occlusion from geometry we already have.
  • The plumbing: varint-packed binary tiles, shader warm-up behind the loader, per-chunk culling, perf assertions in tests.
  • The map: one cached layer for radar and big map, with a route line.
  • TSL, eventually. One shader language for WebGL2 and WebGPU, and compute for crowds.

Neukölln, alive

A whole year of Kiez in a browser tab. Real streets and real ground, a clock that runs, people with somewhere to be, and work you pick up on the corner. Small enough to know every Späti; deep enough to live in for a summer.

🌙

A day that turns

Berlin time and Berlin weather if you want them. Spätis open by their OSM opening_hours, Weserstraße fills after ten, Sunday goes quiet, the Feld at sunset.

🚲

Work the Kiez

Jobs, not missions. Bike courier between real restaurants and real doors, a shift on the M41 down Sonnenallee, a night of taxi. Pfand grows into owning a Späti.

🚨

Heat, Berlin style

Kontrolleure on the U8, Ordnungsamt on your double-parking, Polizei for the stolen Golf. Lose them through the Durchfahrten we already map.

🚇

Underground

Every U-Bahn entrance is a tear like the shop doors. Ride U7 or U8 between stations: fast travel you can see out of.

🧍

People with lives

Schedules, conversations, dives. The Späti owner remembers your Pfand; the wedding convoy is someone's actual wedding.

🗺️

All of Berlin

Berlin publishes LoD2 buildings with real roofs, the street-tree register and DGM1. Stream them as packed tiles; any OSM city as the fallback.

🎛️

Kiez makers

The .song format and the jukebox editor are the template: jobs, shop interiors and convoys as text files with live editors. New York already has remixes; we can have a scene.

👥

Together

A shared Kiez: other players' bikes on the street, ghost Pfand runs, a convoy you drive in with friends.
